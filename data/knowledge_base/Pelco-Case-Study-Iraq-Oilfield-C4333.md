Oil & Gas Company T urns to ExSite 
IP Explosion-proof Systems for 
Enhanced Iraq Oilfield Security
Issue
A mid-east based oil and gas company, who has had 
a successful Pelco video surveillance system in place 
for several years, wanted to expand their coverage of 
a large oilfield in Iraq. With existing Pelco Spectra and 
Esprit systems in place, managers now needed to add 
PTZ camera systems specifically designed to deal with 
the harsh oilfield environment.
Solution
More than 70 ExSite explosionproof, integrated 
positioning systems were added to the system and 
installed throughout the oilfield for optimum coverage. 
Facility managers and installers appreciated the system’s 
flexibility with multiple compression formats, excellent
lowlight performance capabilities, and the ability to install 
the systems for upright or inverted operation.
Result
The ExSite IP camera systems provide the image quality, 
performance and reliability the customer needed for 
this very challenging, mission-critical environment. The 
customer now has a total of 150 Pelco cameras in place, 
and they are happy with everything Pelco has provided.
Featuring both IP and analog capabilities, ExSite 
integrated camera systems have been adopted by 
leading integrators and end users around the globe. 
ExSite is the camera system of preference for the world’s 
most demanding video surveillance applications.
