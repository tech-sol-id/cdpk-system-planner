Signal Splitter
Professional device for indoor radio signal coverage
Introduction
The Signal Splitter is a RF signal distribution and equalization device designed specifically for
communication system, featuring easy design and stable performance.
It provides stable and high-quality signal processing for RF distribution of uplink and downlink signals
in base station. By stripping small signals from the Base station, connect the OMU，add base station cover
ranger, and it is compatible with multi-carrier capacity systems. It has the advantages of low standing
wave, port isolation, power handling, and heat dissipation functions.

Description
The Signal Splitter equally splits the downlink/uplink RF signals into multiple outport, which are then
sent to the OMU.
