ORU（Fiber Optical Remote Unit）
Professional device for indoor radio signal coverage
Introduction
The ORU is designed for high-capacity two-way radio systems. this ORU utilizes the latest digital
intermediate frequency processing and frequency selection technology to better prevent transmission
interference between adjacent frequencies, providing a high-quality signal boost for concur-rent
communication and longer transmission coverage. It can easily achieve high-quality signal transmission
up to 20 kilometers.
Information
Name: ORU
Model: FMTCS-LRF/F
Spec.code DORU400CH.RI1
Description
The ORU receive and acquire the optical signal transmitted by the OMU.And demodulates the optical
signal back into digital RF signal, restores it to the RF signal.The ORU amplifies RF signals and extends
coverage area.
Meanwhile, the ORU will process the walkie-talkie signal in the same way and transmit it to the OMU
according to the same principle.
