Introduction
The Indoor Antenna is designed for two-way radio communication system indoor signal coverage.
Featuring a minimalist design and easy installation, it is suitable for recessed ceiling mounting or surface
mounting, and blends well with indoor environments. Compact and lightweight, the antenna delivers high
gain and is ideal for small-area indoor coverage.Adopting computer-aided design, it meets the standing
wave performance requirements across a wide operating frequency band.
