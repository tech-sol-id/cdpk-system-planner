Issue
A large water filtration plant in South Korea which 
processes and supplies purified water to almost a 
million local citizens, needed to expand their video 
system for enhanced security. Facility managers 
wanted to enhance their system with a unique and 
reliable solution to protect the water from intruders and 
potentially hazardous incidents. The plant’s main facility 
covers a large area, which is not illuminated at night. 
Sarix Thermal imaging provided an excellent solution.
Solution
System managers installed five Sarix TI314 thermal 
imaging camera systems. These five fixed cameras 
provide the wide range of coverage needed for the 
facility, day or night. Additionally, the Pelco suite of 
Video analytics, and the ability to integrate the thermal 
cameras with the Milestone XProtect VMS were very 
desired features.
Sarix TI is the first thermal camera specifically designed 
to be an integrated part of any end-to-end video security 
solution. Completely operable with both Pelco and third-
party video management and recording systems, Sarix 
TI is quite simply, the evolution of thermal imaging. As 
easy to install and use as any traditional dome camera, 
Sarix TI brings advanced thermal imaging capabilities 
to mainstream video security applications. The result is 
an affordable camera system that delivers high-quality 
video as well as the power and flexibility that only comes 
with thermal imaging technology.
