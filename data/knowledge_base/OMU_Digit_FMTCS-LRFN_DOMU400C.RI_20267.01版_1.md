OMU（Fiber Optical Master Unit）
Professional device for indoor radio signal coverage
Introduction
The OMU is designed for two-way radio systems. This OMU utilizes the latest digital intermediate
frequency processing and frequency selection technology to better prevent transmission interference
between adjacent frequencies, providing a high-quality signal boost for concur-rent communication and
longer transmission coverage. It can easily achieve high-quality signal transmission up to 20 kilometers.
Information
Name: OMU
Model: FMTCS-LRF/N
Spec.code: DOMU400C.RI1
Description
The OMU processes the RF signal into digital intermediate frequency signal, then modulates and
converts the processed digital signal into an optical signal, which is transmitted through optical fiber to the
ORU.
