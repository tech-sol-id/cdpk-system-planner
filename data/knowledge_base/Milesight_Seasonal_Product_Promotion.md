### Slide 15
- ENHANCED IMAGE PROCESSING
- ——GLOBAL SHUTTER
- Global shutter technology enhances surveillance capabilities by ensuring comprehensive coverage with a high capture rate. It maintains high-quality images and enables advanced event analysis while reducing the need for manual intervention, improving operational efficiency.
- Rolling Shutter: The image sensor scans row by row, causing slight time differences in exposure. This can lead to distortion in fast-moving scenes or with camera movement.
- Global Shutter: Captures the entire image simultaneously, eliminating distortion and accurately representing fast-moving objects or scenes with motion.
- To get distortion-free image and catch faster vehiles

- ——DUAL IR WAVELENGTH
- Optimize license plate identification by utilizing our optional dual IR wavelengths (850nm and 740nm), enabling precise recognition across different contrast levels and lighting conditions.
- Dual IR Wavelength Options
- 850nm IR Wavelength: 850nm infrared falls within the range that is not visible to the human eye and is often referred to as "non-visible" infrared. It has a longer illumination distance than 740nm  in theory.
- Ideal for: High-contrast/ Non-reflective License Plate
- 740nm IR Wavelength: 740nm infrared wavelength is close to the red region of the visible spectrum and may be slightly visible under certain conditions.
- Ideal for: Low-contrast/ Reflective License Plate
